A Historic Legacy

Home of the First Arbor Day

Villanueva de la Sierra holds a unique place in history as the home of the first "Fiesta del Árbol" (Festival of the Tree) in the world, dating back to 1805. This deep-rooted respect for nature defines our community to this day.

Gastronomy

Flavors of the Land: Olive Oil & Tradition

Villanueva de la Sierra is immersed in the culture of olive oil. Part of the DOP Gata-Hurdes, our "Manzanilla Cacereña" olive oil is liquid gold—renowned for its intense fruitiness and stability.
